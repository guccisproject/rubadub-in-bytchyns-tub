About Us

A small tub, a soggy dog, and a whole lot of suds — here’s how Rubadub in Bytchyns Tub came to be.

How It Started

It began, as these things do, with a dog named Biscuit and a bathtub entirely too small for him. Our founder spent one particularly muddy Gadsden afternoon wrestling Biscuit into the family tub, and somewhere between the third escape attempt and the second bottle of shampoo, a thought landed: surely there’s a better way to do this.

What followed was a very unscientific deep-dive into gentle shampoos, no-slip mats, and towels that actually dry a dog before he shakes water across the whole bathroom. Friends started asking where to find the good stuff. Then friends-of-friends. Eventually “the good stuff” needed a shelf, and the shelf needed a storefront, and Rubadub in Bytchyns Tub was born right here in Gadsden, NC.

What We Believe

We believe tub time should be more rub-a-dub than rigmarole. Every product on our shelves is picked for being gentle, genuinely useful, and boutique-quality — nothing medicated, nothing clinical, just good honest grooming and comfort gear for pets and the people who love them.

We’re not a vet’s office and we don’t play one. We’re your neighborhood bath-and-boutique stop: the place you come for a new brush, a cozy bed, a squeaky duck, or just to talk about how your dog absolutely does not enjoy the hose.

Our Promise
