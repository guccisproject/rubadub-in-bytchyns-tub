Shipping Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how Rubadub in Bytchyns Tub ships orders placed through our website, and describes our local pickup option for customers in and around Gadsden, North Carolina.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day. During busy seasons, processing may take a little longer — we’ll always let you know if there’s a delay.

Shipping Rates & Delivery

  • Flat-rate shipping of $6.95 applies to orders under $60.00.
  • Free standard shipping applies to orders of $60.00 or more (before tax).
  • Estimated delivery time is 3–7 business days after processing, depending on your location.

Shipping costs and estimated delivery windows are calculated and displayed in your cart before you check out.

Local Pickup

Prefer to skip shipping altogether? Choose Pay In-Store / Local Pickup at checkout to reserve your order online and pay when you collect it at our Gadsden, NC location during store hours (Tuesday–Saturday, 10am–6pm).

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, we’ll reach out using the contact information you provided with tracking details where available.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged or never arrives, please contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date so we can help sort it out with the carrier or arrange a replacement.

Sales Tax

North Carolina sales tax (state plus applicable local rate) is calculated and added at checkout for orders shipped within North Carolina, in accordance with North Carolina law.

Questions?

Contact us at contact@rubadubinbytchynstub.shop or (828) 735-6264.
