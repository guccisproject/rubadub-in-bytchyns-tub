Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Rubadub in Bytchyns Tub (“we,” “us,” or “our”), based in North Carolina and shipping nationwide, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects, how it’s used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

This is a static website with no server-side database. Information is collected only in these limited ways:

  • Contact Form: When you use our Contact Us form, your name, email address, and message are handed off to your own email application via a “mailto” link. We never transmit this information to a server we operate as part of that form.
  • Cart & Checkout: Items you add to your cart, and any order details you submit at checkout (name, email, phone, shipping address, and payment method selection), are stored locally in your browser’s localStorage so your cart and order confirmation work correctly. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us automatically.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use browser local storage and, if you accept them, optional analytics/marketing cookies. See our Cookie Notice for details.

How We Use Information

If you email us (including via the contact form or by placing an order and contacting us to fulfill it), we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, fulfill your order, and provide customer service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment Information

At this time, card details entered at checkout are validated in your browser only and are not transmitted to a payment processor or charged — see our Terms & Conditions. If we integrate a third-party payment processor (such as Stripe) in the future, that processor’s own privacy policy will govern how it handles your payment data, and we will update this policy accordingly.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and products are directed at adults purchasing pet care items and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can manage optional cookies at any time using the “Cookie Preferences” button in our site footer, clear your browser’s local storage to remove your cart and stored order history, and contact us to ask what information we may hold from your direct correspondence with us.

North Carolina Residents

We do not sell personal information as defined under applicable law. If you have questions about how your information is handled, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
